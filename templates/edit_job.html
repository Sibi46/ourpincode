💼 What is White Collar?
Jobs done in an office or professional setting — using your knowledge, skills, or qualifications. No heavy physical work involved.
e.g. Software Developer, Teacher, Doctor, Accountant, Manager, Bank Officer
🔧 What is Blue Collar?
Jobs that involve physical work, manual skills, or operating tools and machines — at a site, shop, road, or home.
e.g. Driver, Cook, Electrician, Plumber, Security Guard, Factory Worker
