Our Approach

The Community-to-Market Model

We don't start with advertising. We start with people. Our 7-step model transforms communities into sustainable markets.

01
Find the Community

Identify people who share a need, interest, location or aspiration.

02
Understand the Need

Understand what the community actually wants — not what we assume.

03
Create the Solution

Develop or identify products and services that genuinely solve those needs.

04
Build Engagement

Create conversations, experiences, events and digital interactions.

05
Activate the Market

Connect the community with the product, service or opportunity.

06
Create Advocates

Turn customers into community ambassadors who extend the market.

07
Scale

Use the community network to expand into new markets and opportunities.
